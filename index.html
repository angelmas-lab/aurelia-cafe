Where It All Started

Aurelia Café was started by three girls who wanted to build their own business. We began with interesting and delicious desserts and drinks at affordable prices.

We want Aurelia to be a café and a co-working space. Students and co-workers nearby can come to relax, get their work done and enjoy the vibe. Find us on Sathorn Road, easy to reach by car.
